Pune, July 4, 2026 — The Pune Traffic Police have designated 26 critical waterlogging-prone locations across the city and submitted this list to the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC), requesting immediate preventive action before peak monsoon conditions intensify.

Named waterlogging hotspots include: Shahir Amar Shaikh Chowk, Fatimanagar, Bhairobanala Chowk, Radha Hotel Chowk in Baner, North Main Road in Mundhwa, Tadigutta Chowk, Seven Loves Chowk, the stretch beneath Ruby Hall Metro Station, Gunjan Talkies, and Rajaram Bridge, along with the Bund Garden Area where low-lying stretches are highly vulnerable to waterlogging.

Classification system: Deputy Commissioner of Police (Traffic) Sandip Bhajibhakare explained that locations have been categorized into four severity grades — A+, A, B+, and B — with A+ and A representing the most critical areas. This classification was derived from analyzing emergency calls during the 2025 monsoon season. The assessment considered two primary factors: water accumulation depth and traffic volume. Many sites are vulnerable due to sewage chambers, natural streams, or water channels beneath bridges.

Requested interventions: police requested the PMC deploy portable and mud pumps to quickly drain flooded roads, aiming to restore normal traffic flow and enhance commuter safety during heavy rainfall.
