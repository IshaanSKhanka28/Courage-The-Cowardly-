[Article "Details of Zonal Measures Taken in Response to Rainfall within BBMP Limits", posted May 22, 2025, byline Pramesh S Jain]

Chief Commissioner Inspects Silk Board Junction, Issues Instructions via Virtual Meeting to Officials. Health Camp Organized in Sai Layout.

The city has been experiencing continuous rainfall over the past two days. The Meteorological Department has issued a 'Yellow Alert' for heavy rain accompanied by strong winds from May 20 to May 22. In view of the ongoing rainfall across all eight zones of BBMP, officials have been instructed to remain alert and actively address any arising issues. Citizens are advised to stay cautious and are encouraged to report problems by calling the BBMP helpline number–1533.

Mahadevapura Zone: Zonal Commissioner Ramesh visited Sai Layout and conducted on-site inspection. The water levels have reduced by approximately 80%, and if there is no further rain today, the area is expected to be fully drained. Slabs on roadside drains have been removed, and water is being cleared using JCBs and staff to ensure free flow through cleaned drains. Currently, 1 JCB, 2 tractors, 2 HP pump sets, and 16 staff are deployed.

Bommanahalli Zone: In areas such as HSR Layout and Anugraha Layout, which experienced waterlogging, pump sets have been used to drain the water.

West Zone: Zonal Commissioner Suralkar Vikas Kishor inspected KR Market and Belimath Road along with officials. Water stagnation was observed in the parking area of KR Market and steps have been taken to clear it.

South Zone: At the Silk Board Junction, rainwater is not draining effectively due to the presence of a storm water drain and metro construction works. Transport Minister and local MLA Ramalinga Reddy, along with Chief Commissioner Maheshwar Rao, visited the site for inspection. Due to high water levels in the storm water drain, Silk Board Junction has been flooded. Officials have been directed to initiate restoration work on the storm water drain and coordinate with the BMRCL to take immediate preventive actions.

RR Nagar Zone: On Andrahalli Main Road near Janani School, a retaining wall collapsed, damaging a roadside drain and causing an electric pole to fall. In the Jawaregoudanadoddi area, side drains are blocked, resulting in waterlogging.

75 Lakes Filled Across City: Out of 183 lakes within BBMP limits, 75 have already reached capacity due to the ongoing rainfall. Cleaning operations are being conducted at the outflow points (sluices) of these lakes.

Clearing of Fallen Trees: Reports were received of 25 trees and 44 large branches fallen due to the rain. Of these, 10 trees and 25 branches have been cleared so far.

Further Actions being taken by BBMP: For the fiscal year 2024-25, the Government of Karnataka has allocated Rs. 2,000 crores to BBMP under the "Karnataka Water Security and Disaster Resilience Initiative." Under this scheme, 173.90 km of remaining earthen storm water drains will be upgraded with RCC retaining walls. Additional scientific methods will be implemented by KSNDMC, and measures will be taken to remove encroachments along storm water drains.
