The Pune Municipal Corporation has identified 73 new waterlogging spots in June 2025, signaling an escalating urban flood crisis. Between 2023 and 2025, the number of flood-prone locations has increased substantially.

Financial and support measures: PMC seeks ₹250 crore under the National Disaster Management Plan (NDMP). The National Disaster Management Authority allocated ₹50 crore over five years. Pune ranks among only seven Indian cities receiving dedicated NDMA flood resilience support.

Infrastructure and response: The city has activated 71 shelter facilities and established 24x7 flood control rooms across all 15 ward offices and the main headquarters. Deployed resources include dewatering pumps, jetting machines, JCBs, rubber boats, and utility vehicles.

Contributing factors: according to officials, "uncontrolled urbanisation and poor drainage maintenance" enable even moderate rainfall of 35-40 mm to cause flooding. River encroachments have reduced water capacity, causing floods at historically lower dam release levels.

Emergency contact numbers for residents to report waterlogging: 020-25501269, 020-25506800, 020-67801500. Residents are encouraged to report waterlogging immediately to the nearest control room.
