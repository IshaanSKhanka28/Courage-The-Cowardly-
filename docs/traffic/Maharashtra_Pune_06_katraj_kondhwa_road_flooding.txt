Date: May 20, 2025. Location: Katraj, Pune (specifically affecting the Katraj-Kondhwa Road).

Heavy rainfall that began Tuesday afternoon led to significant traffic disruptions on the busy Katraj-Kondhwa Road. Water accumulated at specific locations including Mauli Nagar and Gokul Nagar, creating waterlogged stretches that severely hindered vehicle movement.

Commuters faced extended delays due to multiple factors: waterlogged stretches, reduced visibility, and slow-moving traffic.

Authorities advised caution, urging motorists to "exercise caution while traveling in the area."

Note: this article does not provide rainfall measurements in millimeters, only a descriptive account of the heavy rainfall event and its traffic consequences.
