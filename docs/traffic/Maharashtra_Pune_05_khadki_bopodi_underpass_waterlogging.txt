On September 20, 2025, intense rainfall caused three rail underpasses along the Khadki-Bopodi stretch to flood, disrupting traffic for approximately 30 minutes.

Affected underpasses: the government hatcheries underpass (Range Hills Road connection), which accumulated knee-deep water over several days; the Khadki police station underpass, experiencing potholes and seepage issues; and the Bopodi underpass, requiring resurfacing and structural repairs.

A frequent user of this route stated: "The condition of the carriageway hasn't improved in four months. Water seeps continuously into the underpass, and two-wheeler riders are especially cautious during heavy rains because the road becomes treacherous."

Another commuter reported: "Many vehicles got stuck due to waterlogging and potholes at the Khadki underpass. Pedestrians are hesitant to pass through the flooded area near the government hatcheries. These ageing facilities need urgent revamping."

Official response: the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) and Khadki Cantonment Board are reportedly planning a joint widening and upgrade project, though work has not yet commenced. PMC officials assured residents that repairs would begin soon, but commuters remain skeptical about the durability of temporary fixes, which historically last only two weeks.
