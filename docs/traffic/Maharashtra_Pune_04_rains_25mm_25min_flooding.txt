Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad experienced severe weather on Thursday afternoon with intense precipitation and strong winds. Meteorological data recorded approximately 25 millimeters of rainfall within just 25 minutes, with wind speeds between 28-35 kilometers per hour.

Impact and damage: the sudden downpour triggered widespread flooding across multiple neighborhoods. Jangli Maharaj Road became severely waterlogged, while residential areas like Balajinagar suffered water infiltration into homes. Within 90 minutes, waterlogging appeared in low-lying zones near Bal Gandharva Rang Mandir and Hinjewadi. Strong gusts uprooted numerous trees and damaged vehicles throughout the affected areas. Commuters sought shelter along roadsides as conditions deteriorated rapidly.

Geographic extent: heavy rainfall impacted Sinhagad Road, Dhankawadi, Katraj, Hadapsar, Lohegaon, and Kharadi. Hailstorms occurred in Katraj-Satara Road, Kothrud, and Karve Nagar regions. Weather radar indicated forming thunderstorms over western and northwestern sections, including Baner, Aundh, Sus, Hinjewadi, and Wakad.

The city experienced recurring afternoon downpours since Monday, displaying monsoon-like behavior during the typically hot summer season.
