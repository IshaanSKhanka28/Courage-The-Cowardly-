Mumbai Rain Impact - July 1, 2026

Andheri Subway Closure: The Andheri Subway was shut down due to waterlogging following heavy overnight rainfall. This represented a recurring issue, as the same corridor had flooded on both Monday and Tuesday before briefly reopening. Authorities reclosed it after fresh showers triggered renewed water accumulation.

Rainfall Measurements: During a 24-hour period ending at 8 a.m., Mumbai recorded moderate precipitation with variations by region:
- Eastern suburbs: 40.62 mm (highest)
- Island city: 26.03 mm
- Western suburbs: 22.47 mm

Weather Forecast: The India Meteorological Department predicted "continued heavy showers and intermittent thunderstorms" across Mumbai and surrounding areas, urging residents to "avoid unnecessary travel and stay clear of weak structures and heavily wooded areas."

Additional Disruptions: Multiple fallen trees blocked major roads including DN Nagar and SV Road in Andheri. A two-vehicle collision was reported on the Eastern Freeway due to low visibility. An 11-year-old student was fatally injured when a tree collapsed onto a school bus in Chembur.

School Status: The Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation had not issued formal closure notifications, though parents were advised to contact their respective institutions directly for updates.
