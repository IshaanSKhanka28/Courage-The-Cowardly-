Mumbai Heavy Rain Red Alert: BMC Advisory Summary
Date: July 6, 2026
Alert Level: Red Alert issued by Indian Meteorological Department

Weather Conditions: Extremely heavy rainfall across Greater Mumbai region; wind speeds 80-90 km/h.

BMC Official Statement: The civic body urged residents to "avoid stepping out of their homes unless necessary" and stated that "Your safety and vigilance are of utmost importance."

Key Advisories: Maintain distance from trees, deteriorating structures, hoardings, and electrical poles. Do not park vehicles beneath trees. Avoid beaches and low-lying areas. Follow only official channels for information; disregard unverified reports.

Impact Details: Schools and colleges declared closed. Central, Western, and Harbour train lines disrupted due to waterlogging. Over 400 tree-fall complaints received citywide. Multiple areas submerged, including Andheri Milan Subway, Kurla, and Chunabhatti. Fatalities reported in Chembur, Kurla, and Aarey Colony.

Emergency Contact: BMC helpline number 1916.

Response: Approximately 15,000 municipal officers and agency personnel deployed for ground-level operations and coordination.
