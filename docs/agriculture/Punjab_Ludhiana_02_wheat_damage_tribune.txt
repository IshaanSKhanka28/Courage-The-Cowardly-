Wheat Crop Damage in Ludhiana

Location: Ludhiana and Ropar districts, Punjab. Date of incident: late March 2023 (rainfall on March 25-26).

Damage extent: Over 25,000 hectares affected in Ludhiana district; approximately 30,000 hectares damaged in Ropar district (33-75% damage assessed). Total wheat cultivation in Ludhiana: 2.43 lakh hectares.

Cause: Unseasonal rainfall and gusty winds during the hard dough stage when wheat was at maturity.

Impact details: Crop lodging (flattening) caused significant losses. "Harvesting process will be delayed by 15 to 20 days," according to the Chief Agriculture Officer. Expected yield decrease of approximately 10%. Mustard and barley crops also damaged in Ropar.

Affected villages: Aatiana, Rajoana Kalan, Rajoana Khurd, Sudhar, Akalgarh, Raikot, and Rauni.

Positive note: Farmers using PAU's surface seeding technique experienced less lodging than those using traditional methods, demonstrating the effectiveness of this approach for weather resilience.
