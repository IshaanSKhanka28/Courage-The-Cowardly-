Ludhiana Rainfall and Agricultural Advisory

Rainfall data: Ludhiana received 79.4 mm of rainfall in 24 hours, bringing August's total to 170 mm - exceeding previous years' figures (160.6 mm in 2024, 79.6 mm in 2023, and 53.4 mm in 2022). The India Meteorological Department warned of additional heavy showers ahead.

PAU advisory for farmers: Punjab Agricultural University experts issued guidance for cultivators, emphasizing water management in fields. Dr MS Bhullar, Director of Extension Education at PAU, cautioned that "Standing water can damage root systems and increase the risk of fungal infections." Farmers were instructed to maintain proper drainage and prevent water accumulation lasting more than 24 hours, particularly in paddy-growing regions.

Additional impacts: Several schools shifted to online classes due to waterlogged roads and student safety concerns. A district survey was underway to assess potential crop damage. Residents experienced significant traffic disruptions, with commuters reporting knee-deep water on major roads.
