AMFU LUDHIANA (Amritsar, Taran Taran, Kapurthala, Jalandhar, Ludhiana, Fathegarh Sahib, Patiala):

RICE (Heading): "Irrigate the paddy field two days after the ponded water has infiltrated into the soil and stop irrigation two weeks before maturity for easy harvesting and timely sowing of next rabi season crop. The rice fields showing more than 5 per cent dead hearts... due to stem borer attack, should be sprayed with recommended pesticides."

MAIZE (Grain filling and Maturity): "Maintain adequate water supply particularly at tasseling and silking stages. Stress at these stages causes considerable losses in yield. Adjust irrigation as per weather."

SUGARCANE (Grand Growth): "Weather is likely to remain favourable for prop up the sugarcane crop by using trash-twist method. Irrigate the crop at regular intervals for getting higher yields. Rogue out the canes affected by red rot and wilt."

GENERAL ADVISORY (applies to all crops in this zone): "Farmers are advised to withhold irrigation in crops, vegetables, and orchards during next 4-5 days. Ensure proper drainage in fields to avoid waterlogging and promptly remove excess rainwater after showers. Application of fertilizers and plant protection chemicals recommended in crop-specific advisories should only be applied when weather become clear."

Extended range forecast for Punjab (18-24 September 2026): "Rainfall likely to be below normal over the state during the period... Light to moderate rainfall likely at isolated places on 18th and 19th and weather likely to be dry thereafter in the state."
