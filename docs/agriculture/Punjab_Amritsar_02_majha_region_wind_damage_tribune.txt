Wheat Crop Damage in Majha Region

Date & location: March 17-18, 2023; Amritsar and Tarn Taran districts in Punjab's Majha region.

Weather damage: Heavy rainfall paired with high-velocity winds caused extensive crop damage on the night of March 17. The article notes that while rain causes limited harm, "the wind can cause incurable damage to the crop at this stage," particularly affecting crops in the earing (mature grain) phase.

Affected villages: Raspulpur, Chohla Sahib, Dhunn Dhai Wala, Kambo, Jaohal Dhai Wala, Gharka, Chamba, Karmunwala, Mundapind, Rattoke, Darajke, Gazal, Machhike, Dasuwal, Bhura, Maddar, Mathra, and Khemkaran.

Damage extent: The flattening of wheat crops created two cascading problems: reduced yield and diminished grain quality. Early-sown, taller wheat varieties sustained more damage than shorter varieties. Farmers who had recently irrigated (as recommended by Punjab Agricultural University) experienced particularly severe losses.

Response: Kisan Mazdoor Sangharsh Committee activists demanded government compensation and a damage survey, urging Chief Minister Bhagwant Mann's intervention.
